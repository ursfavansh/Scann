Food grades use a simplified nutrient formula based on available sugar, saturated fat, salt, energy, fibre, protein, and processing data per 100g. Sources are queried together and compatible nutrition fields are merged. If nutrition data is insufficient, no score is shown. This is not an official regulatory rating — always check the pack for full nutrition and allergen information.
